Meet HATCH — my little virtual pet project

Link: https://hatch-pet.samfirn.workers.dev

It's an early prototype in the spirit of Tamagotchi/Digimon. Just tap the link
on your phone — it works in any browser.

TIP: add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app (full screen, works
offline):
  - iPhone (Safari): Share button -> "Add to Home Screen"
  - Android (Chrome): menu (three dots) -> "Install app" / "Add to Home screen"

Then:

  1. Pick a pace (Authentic = classic, needs you a few times a day; or Gentle),
     then help the egg hatch and name your pet.
  2. Look after it over the next few days — feed it, play games, clean up, turn
     the light off at night so it sleeps. It keeps living even when the app's
     closed, so check in a few times a day.
  3. Watch what it becomes — how you raise it decides which creature it
     grows into.
  4. Fun extras: the paint button changes the screen art style, shuffle changes
     the device case, and the pin keeps a case you like.

What I'd love to know:
  - First impression — did it "click" without me explaining it?
  - Did it feel too needy, or too quiet?
  - Anything confusing, broken, or that made you smile?
  - Would you actually keep one of these going?

It saves your pet on your phone only. Totally fine to start over (the reset
button). Thanks for trying it!
